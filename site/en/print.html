Every piece has a reason.

No deliverable enters your case file for volume. This is what each one is — and what it does for your case.

The business

Eligibility evaluation

A diagnosis of your profile under real USCIS criteria, before you invest. From day one you know whether your case is viable and what it lacks.

Company formation and bank account

Your LLC or Corp operating in the United States. The project stops being an intention: it exists, operates and bills within the economy.

Professional brand

Logo, brand book, website, domain and corporate email. When the officer looks up your project, they find a real, coherent company.

Business plan

Business model, market, projections and jobs to be created. The backbone of your project's substantial merit.

ICP and active campaign

Your ideal client defined and a campaign generating real prospects. Demand is proven with data, not declared.

The case file

Self-petition letter

Your formal statement as a self-petitioner: who you are, what you propose and why it serves the United States, in the language USCIS expects to read.

Petition Letter and I-140 with quality control

The legal argument that ties all the evidence to the precedent's criteria, and the filing-ready form with double review. It is the first thing the officer reads.

Expert letters

Specialists in your industry substantiate the importance of your project. USCIS weighs who says it as much as what is said.

Recommendation letters

Your track record told by third parties with names and titles. Experience becomes evidence, not self-praise.

Filing and adjustment of status

We file the case, manage the adjustment through to the Green Card and answer any request by raising the evidentiary bar.

Family residency

Adjustment of status for your spouse and children, included. The Green Card belongs to the family, not just to you.

The authority

Government Evaluation Package

Written endorsements from governors' offices, mayors' offices and government agencies on your project. The hardest evidence to obtain — and the hardest to ignore.

Press, Knowledge Panel and Times Square

Allied media publish your project, Google recognizes you as an entity and your brand reaches New York screens. Verifiable public authority.

Published book with ISBN

Your knowledge turned into a registered work. Few pieces say “expert” with such force — and it remains yours forever.

Patent application

Formal protection filing for your method or innovation. Hard evidence that your contribution is original and holds value beyond you.

Econometric study and market validation

Your project's impact in numbers: jobs, economic spillover, real demand. National importance goes from adjective to figure.

Case study

Real results of your method, rigorously documented. Applied proof that what you propose already works.

Mobile app or software
